I am **Siyuan Chen**, a recent physics graduate from **Shanghai Jiao Tong University** with broad interests in **computational physics**, **computer graphics**, and **machine learning**.

My work is motivated by a simple question: how can we build numerical and computational methods that remain faithful to physical principles while also being practical for real-world simulation and modeling?

Background
======

I received my **B.Sc. in Physics** from **Shanghai Jiao Tong University** in **June 2025**. During my undergraduate studies, I also spent **September 2024 to December 2024** as an exchange student at the **University of Toronto**.

Current Work
======

Since **September 2025**, I have been working as a **Research Assistant** at **The University of Hong Kong** in the **Taku Komura Group**, within **AI, Robotics and Visual Computing** at the **School of Computing and Data Science**.

Next Step
======

I am an **incoming Ph.D. student** at **The University of British Columbia**.

Research Interests

Research

Learning the dynamics
of the physical world.

I'm a CS Ph.D. student at the University of British Columbia, advised by Prof. Peter Yichen Chen.

My research develops AI foundation models for protein structure, interaction, and dynamics, combining geometric deep learning, physics-grounded priors, and large-scale molecular dynamics data.

I'm particularly interested in physically valid biomolecular generation, scalable protein–protein interaction discovery, and Transformer-based neural simulators that learn protein conformational dynamics from MD trajectories. More broadly, I work on neural surrogate models for physical dynamics, at the intersection of computer graphics, scientific computing, and machine learning.

I graduated from Shanghai Jiao Tong University with a B.S. in Physics and a minor in Computer Science.

Before my Ph.D., I interned at SJTU DA Lab with Prof. Xubo Yang and Hui Wang, collaborated remotely with Bo Zhu, and worked at DMCV with Prof. Ran Yi. In fall 2024, I was an exchange student at the University of Toronto, working with Zhecheng Wang and Prof. Eitan Grinspun.
